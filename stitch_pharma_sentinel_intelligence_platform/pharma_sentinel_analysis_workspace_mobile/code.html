Molecular Pathway Provenance

Fluconazole non-competitively occludes the active heme iron cavity of hepatic cytochrome P450 2C9, preventing metabolic oxidation of the active S-warfarin stereoisomer. Unbound S-warfarin plasma fraction surges within 48 hours of co-administration.

Clinical Intervention Protocol

Avoid concomitant administration. If antifungal therapy is mandatory, select topical or non-azole alternative (e.g., Caspofungin). Alternatively, preemptively reduce Warfarin dose by 50-75% with daily international normalized ratio tracking.
