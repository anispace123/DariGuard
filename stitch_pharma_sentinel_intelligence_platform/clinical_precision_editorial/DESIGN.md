## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intellectual rigor, uncompromising clarity, and quiet gravitas of premier academic and biomedical research institutes. It rejects ephemeral consumer interface trends—eschewing playful micro-interactions, decorative drop shadows, saturated novelty gradients, and blurred glassmorphism—in favor of structural discipline, institutional authority, and high-density editorial legibility.

The platform speaks directly to pharmacologists, clinical research scientists, chief medical officers, and regulatory bodies. Every screen functions like an interactive, living research monograph: confident typographic hierarchies, balanced tabular information architectures, and deliberate, restrained chromatic accents.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses classic Swiss structural grid discipline with the warmth of curated scholarly publications. Generous negative space balances information-dense multi-drug interaction matrices, pharmacokinetic pathways, and metabolic contraindication models. The emotional tone is authoritative, analytical, calm, and unmistakably decisive.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate academic baseline with a foundational canvas of warm institutional paper whites (`#FBFBFA` and `#F7F7F5`). Typography is anchored by deep charcoal ink (`#111418`), delivering archival contrast without the digital harshness of absolute black.

Primary chromatic weight belongs to deep scientific navy (`#1E3A5F`), projecting intellectual stability and institutional weight. A secondary clinical teal (`#0D7A75`) provides specialized focus for affirmative data states, primary metabolic enzymes (CYP450 pathways), and mild/supported interaction statuses. 

Severity indicators are codified with clinical precision rather than alarming neon values:
- **Severe Contraindication:** Deep cinnabar crimson (`#C93B2B`)
- **Moderate / Cautionary Interaction:** Burnished amber (`#D97706`)
- **Mild / Documented Interaction:** Restrained clinical teal (`#0D7A75`)

Structural lines, data cell delimiters, and section divides rely exclusively on crisp hairline dividers (`#E2E4E1`). Interactive hover states use subtle tonal shifts rather than dramatic color jumps, ensuring visual serenity across long diagnostic sessions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial prestige of **Newsreader** with the structural clarity of **Work Sans**.

- **Newsreader** governs macro-level navigation, primary research headlines, executive analytical summaries, and long-form literature narratives. Its optical balance honors the tradition of leading biomedical periodicals, imbuing scientific findings with composure and historical permanence.
- **Work Sans** controls high-density data matrices, molecular identifiers, dosage indicators, interactive states, and micro-labels. With its clean humanist proportions and robust rendering at small sizes, it provides immediate readability across dense pharmacokinetic parameter tables.

All uppercase metadata labels (such as `label-sm` for chemical IDs, ATC codes, and evidence tier tags) feature generous letter spacing (+0.04em to +0.06em) to guarantee immediate scanability across dense interaction sheets. Tabular numerals (`tnum`) should be enabled for all dosage values, clearance rates, and confidence intervals.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural, 12-column grid system calibrated for analytical rigor and complex side-by-side data comparisons.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, 3rem outer margins, 1.5rem column gutters. Primary multi-compound comparison matrices span 8 to 9 columns, reserving a 3 to 4 column sticky rail for pharmacokinetic mechanism details, metabolic pathways, and citation trails.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Secondary details collapse into expandable structural drawers beneath primary compound blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 1rem margins, 1rem gutters. Dense clinical tables transition into modular, card-based interaction digests with persistent severity color bands.

Vertical rhythm is governed by a consistent 4px baseline sub-unit (`0.25rem`). Section boundaries must rely on structured negative space (`space-xl`) paired with 1px hairline dividers rather than elevated containers. Padding within clinical panels remains disciplined to maximize data density without visual friction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through planar layering, tonal contrast, and structural boundaries rather than artificial drop shadows or physical metaphors.

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBFA` serves as the universal backdrop.
   - **Structural Panels:** `#FFFFFF` separates actionable dossiers and compound workspaces.
   - **Secondary Groupings:** `#F7F7F5` isolates peripheral data sets, metadata sidebars, and citation footnotes.

2. **Hairline Partitioning:**
   Depth is articulated by clean 1px borders using `#E2E4E1`. Panels do not float; they sit flush against adjacent cells within a rigid editorial grid, echoing the clean layout of scholarly tables.

3. **Restrained Floating Elevation:**
   For transient elements such as drug lookup autocomplete dropdowns, multi-compound filter menus, and clinical citation tooltips, elevation is achieved via a crisp 1px border (`#E2E4E1`) coupled with an ultra-diffused, desaturated shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.06)`. Heavy blurs, saturated glow effects, and multi-layered drop shadows are strictly prohibited.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing minimal 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radii for functional components like input fields, buttons, and alert pills. Larger structural modules, analytical cards, and tabular containers maintain precise 90-degree corners (0px radius) to reinforce the system's architectural precision and grid discipline.

Interactive chips and severity indicator badges use small 4px radii—pill-shaped containers and rounded bubbles are deliberately avoided to maintain a serious, scholarly character.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid scientific navy background (`#1E3A5F`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, vertical padding `0.625rem`, horizontal padding `1.25rem`. Typography: `label-lg`. On hover: `#2B4C7E`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#1E3A5F`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px solid hairline border (`#E2E4E1`), text `#111418`. Hover: `#F7F7F5` surface tint.
- **Destructive / High-Severity Alert:** Transparent background with 1px solid `#C93B2B` border, text `#C93B2B`. Hover: `#C93B2B` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Badges & Severity Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 22px, padding 2px 8px, 2px radius, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Severe:** `#C93B2B` solid 1px border, 10% opacity `#C93B2B` tint background, bold `#C93B2B` text.
- **Moderate:** `#D97706` solid 1px border, 10% opacity `#D97706` tint background, bold `#D97706` text.
- **Mild / Documented:** `#0D7A75` solid 1px border, 10% opacity `#0D7A75` tint background, bold `#0D7A75` text.

### Inputs & Compound Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#E2E4E1`. Radius: `0.25rem`. Padding: `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Text: `body-md` in `#111418`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1E3A5F` without outer halo glows.
- Helper labels: Positioned above input in `label-md`, color `#5A6578`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 16px × 16px. Border: 1.5px solid `#5A6578`. Radius: 2px (checkbox), 50% (radio).
- Checked State: Solid `#1E3A5F` fill with crisp white vector checkmark. No outer drop shadows.

### Cards & Dossier Panels
- White canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline border (`#E2E4E1`) and square corners (0px radius).
- Header zone: Divided by a 1px bottom border (`#E2E4E1`), incorporating a category label in `label-md` and title in `headline-sm` or `headline-md`.
- Body: Generous internal padding (`space-lg`), segmented by horizontal hairlines for structured clinical metrics (e.g., CYP3A4 substrate status, AUC percentage change).

### Data Tables & Interaction Matrices
- Table header: Height 36px, background `#F7F7F5`, text `label-md`, border-bottom 1px solid `#111418`.
- Table rows: Minimum height 48px, border-bottom 1px solid `#E2E4E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Alternating row zebra fills are avoided; hover highlights use `#FBFBFA`.
- Numerical data: Aligned right using monospace/tabular figures.
